\section*{Ethics Statement}
\label{sec:ethics}
The measurements run local software workloads and involve no human participants or personal data. The REST runtime performs no kernel writes; the experimental adapter changes only its owned child thread's timer slack and affinity, restores both settings, and performs no host-wide sysctl writes. Broader interventions require an isolated testbed, documented rollback limits and appropriate authorization. Any future human study requires prospective institutional review and informed consent.

AI assistants were used for writing and debugging code, for drafting and restructuring prose, and for locating and checking references; the authors reviewed all generated content and verified every reference against its publisher or arXiv record. The explanation claims were annotated by an author, not by an AI assistant. Every reported number is produced by released scripts from raw logs whose SHA-256 hashes are recorded, so that it can be recomputed independently; no generated value substitutes for an unavailable observation.
