Step 1 of 3 · Information and consent

Reading AI-written technical advice

You will read a short recommendation that an AI advisor wrote about choosing a computer configuration. Then you answer six yes/no questions about the measurements behind it and say how much you trust the recommendation. It takes about 10 minutes.

Some details of the study are explained only at the end, because knowing them first would change how you read. We store your answers, whether and when you open the measurements, and how long the task takes, under an opaque account identifier. We do not store your name or e-mail address. Taking part is voluntary, and you may stop at any time before submitting.

Researcher and ethics board contacts are on the information sheet you received.
