\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
SemantOS is a research prototype for inspectable, knowledge-grounded kernel-tuning proposals and independent bundle gating. This revision corrects the risk statements and reports auditable local measurements, role-separated selector replay and actual per-thread restoration. The informed non-LLM selectors show no gain over control, and held-out gate misses caution against treating a calibrated threshold as universal protection. Whether an LLM improves decisions over an equally informed optimizer, and whether staging reduces independently measured harm, remain unresolved despite the added executable evaluation tools.

The separately supplied hardware report is retained for provenance in the appendix. Its unresolved aggregation, missing run-level uncertainty and absent raw logs exclude it from the comparative conclusions of this revision.
