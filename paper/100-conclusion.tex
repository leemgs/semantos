\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
SemantOS is a research prototype for inspectable, knowledge-grounded kernel-tuning proposals with independent bundle gating. We contribute a bundle-level control contract, correctly conditioned risk statements, and a role-separated evaluation protocol with raw-log verification. Applied to real per-thread kernel interventions, the protocol shows that informed non-LLM selectors gain nothing over the default, that a frozen calibrated threshold can miss more unsafe runs than its nominal level under a period shift, and that bounded staged actuation restored the original settings in every execution. Eight LLMs did no better and often misstated the evidence.
