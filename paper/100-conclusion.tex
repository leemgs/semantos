\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
SemantOS is a research prototype for inspectable, knowledge-grounded kernel-tuning proposals and independent bundle gating. This revision corrects the risk statements, separates simulation from measurement, and reports auditable local baselines and actual per-process kernel interventions. Whether the LLM improves decisions over an equally informed optimizer, and whether staged deployment reduces independently measured harm, remain questions for the specified controlled experiments.

A separate author-reported hardware evaluation describes lower tail latency for Graph + LLM and Full SemantOS. Its aggregate estimates are included with their provenance, while unresolved aggregation, missing run-level uncertainty and absent raw logs limit the strength of comparative conclusions.
