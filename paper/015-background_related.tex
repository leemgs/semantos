\section{Background and Related Work}
\label{sec:background}
\paragraph{Kernel interfaces.}
Linux documents controls such as \texttt{vm.swappiness}, \texttt{vm.dirty\_ratio}, and \texttt{vm.dirty\_background\_ratio}~\cite{LinuxVM}. Their availability and semantics must be checked on the actual kernel. A feature name is not necessarily a sysctl: the scheduler's \texttt{WAKE\_AFFINE} feature in Linux 6.4 does not establish the existence of a \texttt{sched\_wake\_affinity} sysctl~\cite{LinuxFeatures}. The current example policy uses a small allowlist of documented memory controls and makes no scheduler-pair performance claim.

\paragraph{Optimization with shared information.}
Black-box optimization need not assume independent parameters. A surrogate can model interactions, and a graph-aware optimizer can receive exactly the evidence given to an LLM. Therefore a comparison that grants offline pairwise-search results only to SemantOS cannot isolate language-model reasoning. Our evaluation protocol includes both cold-start and shared-KB baselines, and accounts separately for offline and online costs. We remove the earlier categorical superiority table, including its inconsistent classification of OS-R1, rather than infer unverified properties of other systems.

\paragraph{Risk control.}
Conformal Risk Control~\cite{Angelopoulos2024CRC} treats expected monotone losses, providing a more appropriate reference than an informal conversion of coverage into conditional acceptance risk. Non-Exchangeable Conformal Risk Control~\cite{Farinhas2024NCRC} addresses weighted, non-exchangeable data; it does not imply that an arbitrary sliding window restores a fixed risk level. Our rank argument below is a restricted class-conditional result, not a replacement for these frameworks or a novel drift theorem.

\paragraph{Explanation faithfulness.}
Provenance lists what context was supplied, not necessarily what influenced a recommendation. ContextCite~\cite{CohenWang2024ContextCite} studies attribution of generated output to context. We adopt context-removal interventions as a planned test of whether cited edges and traces influence the proposed bundle. Operator preference alone cannot establish faithfulness.

\paragraph{Model provenance.}
Meta's Llama 3.1 release comprises 8B, 70B and 405B variants~\cite{Meta2024Llama31}. The prior 13B designation was erroneous. The artifact now requires explicit configuration of an installed model and describes an 8B configuration only as a proposed example, not as the checkpoint used in an experiment.
