\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:background}
Our audit examines a cited rationale at four levels: the decision, the citations, the claims made about the cited evidence, and the reliance of the decision on that evidence. We relate each level to prior work, then turn to LLMs as decision makers and to risk control.

\paragraph{Citation quality and attribution.}
Retrieval-augmented generation conditions outputs on retrieved documents~\cite{Lewis2020RAG}, and attribution research asks whether the resulting text is supported by what it cites. AIS defines attribution to identified sources~\cite{Rashkin2023AIS}; Attributed QA and ALCE benchmark attribution and citation quality~\cite{Bohnet2022AttributedQA,Gao2023ALCE}; RARR revises text to restore attribution~\cite{Gao2023RARR}; and generative search engines leave many statements unsupported by the sources they cite~\cite{Liu2023Verifiability}. These studies ask whether each citation supports its statement. We additionally ask which evidence gets cited at all, and find citations that are valid yet one-sided and concentrated at the start of the evidence list (\S\ref{subsec:citations}).

\paragraph{Claim-level accuracy against data.}
FActScore checks atomic facts against a knowledge source~\cite{Min2023FActScore}. Data-to-text generation has long documented factual errors in text produced from tables~\cite{Wiseman2017DataToDocument}; \citet{Thomson2020GoldStandard} annotate every error in generated basketball summaries against the source data, including incorrect numbers, names and words, and summarization work distinguishes intrinsic from extrinsic hallucination~\cite{Maynez2020Faithfulness,Ji2023HallucinationSurvey}. Statements requiring comparison, superlatives or aggregation over a table are hard both to verify and to generate faithfully~\cite{Chen2020TabFact,Chen2020LogicNLG}. We apply this error-annotation practice to the free-text rationales of instruction-tuned LLMs, where the supplied evidence is small enough for every claim to be checked exactly, and find the same superlative, range and quantifier errors (\S\ref{subsec:claims}).

\paragraph{Faithfulness and reliance.}
Faithfulness must be evaluated separately from plausibility~\cite{Jacovi2020Faithfulness}. ERASER measures the comprehensiveness and sufficiency of rationales by removing them~\cite{DeYoung2020ERASER}; counterfactual and reconstruction tests probe natural-language explanations~\cite{Atanasova2023FaithfulnessTests}; and chain-of-thought explanations can omit the factors that drive a prediction~\cite{Turpin2023Unfaithful} or be tested by intervening on the reasoning~\cite{Lanham2023CoTFaithfulness}. Self-explanation faithfulness varies across models and tasks~\cite{Madsen2024SelfExplanations}, and many such tests measure output-level self-consistency rather than faithfulness to internal computation~\cite{Parcalabescu2024SelfConsistency}; ContextCite attributes generations to context by ablation~\cite{CohenWang2024ContextCite}. Our matched deletion is an ERASER-style comprehensiveness test with a same-kind uncited control and, in the terms of \citet{Parcalabescu2024SelfConsistency}, a self-consistency test (\S\ref{subsec:reliance}).

\paragraph{How models use supplied context.}
Models may ignore context that conflicts with parametric knowledge~\cite{Longpre2021KnowledgeConflicts} or accept coherent counter-evidence while favoring their priors~\cite{Xie2024ChameleonSloth}; irrelevant context distracts them~\cite{Shi2023Distracted}, and information in the middle of long contexts is used less~\cite{Liu2024LostMiddle}. Our evidence ablation examines the complementary case in which the context is decisive and the prior is misleading (\S\ref{subsec:decisions}), and our citation analysis adds a position effect in what models choose to cite.

\paragraph{LLMs as decision makers.}
LLMs can optimize by proposing candidates from past evaluations~\cite{Yang2024OPRO}, and configuration advisors mine tuning hints from manuals~\cite{Trummer2022DBBERT}, prune Bayesian-optimization searches with LLM-extracted knowledge~\cite{Lao2024GPTuner}, generate complete configurations~\cite{Giannakouris2025LambdaTune} or tune Linux kernel build options as agents~\cite{Chen2024AutoOS,Lin2025TuneAgent}; classical tuners use Gaussian processes, reinforcement learning or black-box optimization~\cite{VanAken2017OtterTune,Zhang2019CDBTune,Herodotou2020Survey,Snoek2012BO,Golovin2017Vizier,Lee2022STUN}. These works score the chosen action end to end, whereas we ask what the model adds over a selector given the same evidence and whether its stated reasons are correct; we do not compare with these systems head to head. Following arguments against random or standard splits~\cite{Gorman2019StandardSplits,Sogaard2021RandomSplits} and for reporting matched budgets~\cite{Dodge2019ShowYourWork}, our data roles come from disjoint operating conditions and every baseline has an explicit budget.

\paragraph{Uncertainty and risk control.}
Self-consistency aggregates sampled outputs~\cite{Wang2023SelfConsistency}; LLMs can partly predict their own correctness~\cite{Kadavath2022Know}, verbalized confidence can be better calibrated than token probabilities for RLHF models~\cite{Tian2023JustAsk} yet remains overconfident~\cite{Xiong2024ExpressUncertainty}, and semantic entropy measures uncertainty over meanings~\cite{Kuhn2023SemanticUncertainty}. Selective classification abstains to meet a target risk~\cite{Geifman2017Selective}, and conformal methods give guarantees for language-model outputs~\cite{Quach2024ConformalLM}, their factuality~\cite{Mohri2024ConformalFactuality} and monotone losses~\cite{Angelopoulos2024CRC}, with extensions for non-exchangeable or drifting data~\cite{Farinhas2024NCRC,Barber2023Beyond,Gibbs2021ACI}; none implies that a window reset after a detected change~\cite{Bifet2007ADWIN} restores a fixed risk level. Our rank gate is a restricted class-conditional instance of this family, and we report where its assumptions fail (\S\ref{subsec:gate}).
