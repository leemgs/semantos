\section{Background and Related Work}
\label{sec:background}
\paragraph{Configuration tuning for systems.}
OtterTune~\cite{VanAken2017OtterTune} reuses past workload observations with Gaussian-process models to tune database knobs, and CDBTune~\cite{Zhang2019CDBTune} learns a deep reinforcement-learning policy from trial rewards; a survey~\cite{Herodotou2020Survey} covers rule-based, cost-model, experiment-driven, learned and adaptive tuners for data-processing systems. Bayesian optimization~\cite{Snoek2012BO} and services such as Vizier~\cite{Golovin2017Vizier} are general black-box baselines. For the Linux kernel, STUN~\cite{Lee2022STUN} tunes scheduler parameters with Q-learning for static workloads. None of these methods must assume independent parameters: a surrogate can model interactions, and a graph-aware optimizer can receive exactly the evidence given to an LLM. A comparison that grants offline pairwise-search results only to the language-model system therefore cannot isolate language-model reasoning; our protocol includes both cold-start and shared-KB optimizers and accounts separately for offline and online costs.

\paragraph{Language models for configuration.}
DB-BERT~\cite{Trummer2022DBBERT} mines tuning hints from manuals and refines them with trial runs; GPTuner~\cite{Lao2024GPTuner} uses GPT-extracted knowledge to prune knobs and ranges before coarse-to-fine Bayesian optimization; and $\lambda$-Tune~\cite{Giannakouris2025LambdaTune} generates complete configuration scripts and bounds the cost of evaluating candidates. For operating systems, AutoOS~\cite{Chen2024AutoOS} uses an LLM-driven observe--prune--propose--act--correct loop over Linux kernel build options, and TuneAgent~\cite{Lin2025TuneAgent} trains an LLM agent with rule-based reinforcement learning over Kconfig option groups. These systems target build-time or database configuration and evaluate end-to-end performance. SemantOS instead targets runtime controls and focuses on the enforcement boundary: complete bundles are validated and gated by a component independent of the proposer, and the evaluation separates outcome labels from enforcement decisions. We do not claim to outperform these systems; no head-to-head comparison is reported.

\paragraph{Grounding and uncertainty.}
Retrieval-augmented generation~\cite{Lewis2020RAG} conditions outputs on retrieved documents, and self-consistency~\cite{Wang2023SelfConsistency} aggregates multiple sampled outputs. SemantOS uses both, but treats agreement among samples only as a heuristic score to be calibrated, not as a probability.

\paragraph{Risk control.}
Selective classification~\cite{Geifman2017Selective} abstains to meet a target risk. Conformal Risk Control~\cite{Angelopoulos2024CRC} controls expected monotone losses, and its non-exchangeable extension~\cite{Farinhas2024NCRC}, like weighted conformal prediction~\cite{Barber2023Beyond} and adaptive conformal inference~\cite{Gibbs2021ACI}, addresses drifting data with explicit weights or online updates. None implies that an arbitrary sliding window restores a fixed risk level after a change detected by, e.g., ADWIN~\cite{Bifet2007ADWIN}. Our rank gate is a restricted class-conditional result, not a replacement for these frameworks or a new drift guarantee.

\paragraph{Explanation faithfulness.}
Provenance lists what context was supplied, not necessarily what influenced a recommendation; faithfulness must be evaluated separately from plausibility~\cite{Jacovi2020Faithfulness}. ContextCite~\cite{CohenWang2024ContextCite} attributes generated output to context by ablation. We adopt matched context-deletion interventions to test whether cited edges and traces influence the proposed bundle; operator preference alone cannot establish faithfulness.

\paragraph{Kernel interfaces.}
Linux documents runtime controls such as \texttt{vm.swappiness}, \texttt{vm.dirty\_ratio} and \texttt{vm.dirty\_background\_ratio}~\cite{LinuxVM}, and per-thread controls such as timer slack and CPU affinity~\cite{LinuxTimerSlack,LinuxAffinity}. Availability and semantics must be checked on the running kernel; the example policy uses a small allowlist of documented controls.
