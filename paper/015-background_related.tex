The Linux kernel exposes a vast configuration surface: hundreds of tunable parameters govern memory reclamation (\texttt{vm.swappiness}, \texttt{dirty\_ratio}, \texttt{min\_free\_kbytes}), CPU scheduling (\texttt{sched\_latency\_ns}, \texttt{sched\_min\_granularity\_ns}, \texttt{sched\_wake\_affinity}), interrupt handling, I/O prioritization, NUMA placement, and power management~\cite{Dean2013CACM,Ousterhout2015NSDI,Li2018PVLDB}. These controls let administrators specialize behavior to specific hardware and workload demands.

\paragraph{Traditional Tuning Paradigms.} Static profiles and rule-based heuristics are vulnerable to changing workloads and operator interactions, which limits their usefulness for adaptive tuning and post-hoc diagnosis.

\paragraph{Learning-based optimizers.}
Black-box methods---Bayesian optimization, evolutionary strategies, and reinforcement learning~\cite{Roman2016CEC,Mao2019SIGCOMM,Lee2022AppliedSciences}---propose configurations and update surrogates or policies~\cite{Maas2020IeeeMicro,Roman2019Access}, but typically require hundreds of trials, assume stationary objectives, and offer limited interpretability~\cite{Lee2022AppliedSciences}. SemantOS adds domain-structured, uncertainty-aware safety gates on top of such techniques, enabling stable optimization as workloads and hardware change.

\paragraph{Large Language Models (LLMs) for systems reasoning.}
LLMs have recently shown strong reasoning, multi-objective optimization, and knowledge synthesis abilities~\cite{Brown2020NeurIPS,Touvron2023LLaMA,OpenAI2024GPT4,Lee2025TIST}. Augmented with structured domain knowledge---graphs capturing tunable dependencies, schemas describing hardware/workload characteristics, and telemetry summarizing recent performance---they can interpret complex system states, infer cause--effect relationships, and propose coherent configuration strategies with human-readable justifications that aid operator trust~\cite{Mialon2023AugmentedLM,Chen2024ICML,Jiang2025EMNLP}. The closest such efforts differ from SemantOS in axis and lifecycle: AutoOS~\cite{Chen2024ICML} uses an LLM to search build-time kernel \texttt{.config} options offline, and OS-R1~\cite{Lin2025OSR1} applies RL agents to runtime tuning---neither grounds reasoning in a \emph{typed inter-knob dependency graph}, exposes \emph{calibrated uncertainty}, nor gates changes through a \emph{conformal, staged-rollout safety runtime}. SemantOS targets \emph{online} \texttt{sysctl} adjustment where safety, explanation provenance, and uncertainty-gated automation are first-class.

\paragraph{Summary.}
The complexity of kernel configuration, the shortcomings of static and black-box approaches, and the rise of LLMs for structured reasoning motivate combining telemetry, domain knowledge, and language-model reasoning; since kernel behavior varies across hardware, versions, and workload mixes, naive transfer of a single profile is unreliable. Table~\ref{tab:related-compare} positions SemantOS against AdaptiveKernel~\cite{Lee2022AppliedSciences}, OS-R1~\cite{Lin2025OSR1}, ML-Guided~\cite{Biswas2023SE4SafeML}, and BO/RL~\cite{Herodotou2021CSUR}, highlighting its integration of knowledge-centric reasoning, uncertainty-aware safety, and explainability.
